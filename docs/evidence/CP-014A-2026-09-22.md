# CP-014A evidence — unified worker-loss policy

Implemented a pure, heartbeat-only decision contract in
`app/worker_resource_policy.py`.

- `< 3 minutes`: `ONLINE`.
- `3–<15 minutes`: `OFFLINE`; retain worker resources.
- `>= 15 minutes`: `RECLAIM_ALL_WORKER_RESOURCES` for proxy leases, NKN/Myst
  wallet leases, runtime assignments, and capacity reservations.
- Provider credentials, account-pool membership, EarnApp sticky egress
  ownership, PayPal ownership, and historical evidence are preserved.
- Missing timestamps never trigger destructive reclamation.

The module has no database, network, provider, or client-lease side effects.
Atomic reclamation and stale-worker fencing remain owned by CP-014C/CP-014D.
