## Contract

- Route becomes ready only after proxy/DNS/egress gates pass.
- Route marker TTL is bounded; stale or missing readiness fails closed.
- Missing redsocks, DoH, firewall, IPv6 block, or route marker terminates the
  wrapped provider process. Docker restarts the same container, preserving its
  identity and mounted volumes.
- Repeated route failures write `route_blocked` and restart evidence. No lease
  release, proxy rotation, provider account mutation, or identity recreation is
  performed by the watchdog.
- Sidecar/provider dependency is represented by the shared read-only marker
  volume and health contract; direct fallback remains blocked by nftables and
  the existing proxy route rules.
